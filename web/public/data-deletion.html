Data Deletion

Uyir Health App · Nurse registration (uyirjobs.in) · Last updated 14 November 2026

You can ask us to delete the information you gave us when registering as a nurse with Uyir, at any time and free of charge.

How to ask

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to +91 99524 07365 from the mobile number you registered with, saying you want your data deleted. For example: Please delete my Uyir registration data. If you no longer use that number, tell us your full name, district and the mobile number you registered with.
  2. We may call or message you to confirm the request comes from you, so that nobody else can delete your registration.
  3. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm on WhatsApp or SMS when it is done.

You can also ask your Uyir district office, who will pass your request to us.

What we delete

  • Your name, mobile and WhatsApp numbers, email and address.
  • Your district, taluk, preferred work location and all professional details (qualification, council registration number, employment, experience, work preference, services).
  • All documents you uploaded, including every stored copy.
  • Your bank details (account holder, account number, IFSC, UPI ID).
  • The text of messages we exchanged with you, and notes on your registration.
  • Your personal registration link, which stops working.

What we keep

We keep only a registration ID with no personal details, a record of status changes (for example "verified" and when), and a note of who deleted the record, when and why. This lets us show that your request was carried out, and contains nothing that identifies you. If the law requires us to keep certain records (for example payments already made), we keep only those, for as long as the law requires.

Encrypted backups are removed automatically within 14 days and server logs within 90 days.

Registering again

After deletion you can register again at any time; you will start as a new registration.
