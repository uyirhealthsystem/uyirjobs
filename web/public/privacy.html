Privacy Policy

Uyir Health App · Nurse registration (uyirjobs.in) · Last updated 14 November 2026

In short: we collect your details and documents only to verify your nursing credentials and offer you nursing work in Tamil Nadu. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. You can ask us to delete it at any time: see Data deletion.

Who we are

Uyir Health App ("Uyir", "we") runs this website and our WhatsApp number +91 99524 07365 to register nurses who want to provide home nursing and related services through Uyir. This policy covers the registration website, the SMS and WhatsApp messages we send about registration, and the WhatsApp messages you send us.

What we collect

Contact detailsName, mobile number, WhatsApp number and WhatsApp profile name (if you message us), email, address.
LocationDistrict, taluk and your preferred work location (district, taluk and village).
Professional detailsQualification, Nursing Council registration number, current employment, years of experience, work preference and the services you are interested in.
DocumentsThe files you upload: nursing qualification certificate, Nursing Council registration, a Government ID (Aadhaar, Driving Licence, Passport or ABHA card), and experience or other documents if you choose to add them.
Bank detailsCollected only after your documents are verified: account holder name, account number, IFSC and (optionally) UPI ID, so we can pay you.
Consent and historyYour consent choices, the messages we exchange with you, and a record of changes to your registration (what changed, when and by whom).
Technical dataBasic server logs (time, page requested, response) used to keep the service secure and working. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To register you, verify your qualification, council registration and identity, and ask for corrections if a document is unclear.
  • To contact you about your registration by phone, SMS and WhatsApp (for example: your registration link, reminders, correction requests and the result of verification).
  • To match you with nursing work near your preferred location, and to pay you for services.
  • To keep a record of what was done and by whom, and to meet our legal obligations.

We use your information only with your consent, which you give on the registration form, and for the purposes above. WhatsApp updates are sent only if you message us or agree to receive them, and you can stop them at any time.

Who can see it

  • Uyir staff: the administrator for your district, and our state-level administrators. Staff see only the last 4 digits of your bank account number.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contract: Amazon Web Services (hosting, database and document storage, in the Mumbai region, India) and Meta Platforms (WhatsApp messages you exchange with us).
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell, rent or share your information for marketing.

How we protect it

  • All connections use HTTPS. Your data is stored in India, encrypted at rest.
  • Your bank account number is encrypted separately and never shown in full to staff.
  • Documents are stored privately; staff open them through short-lived links, and every view and review is recorded.
  • Your personal registration link works like a password: please do not share it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your registration is active or you work with Uyir, and as long as needed for our legal and accounting obligations. When you ask us to delete your data, we erase it as described on the Data deletion page. Encrypted backups are kept for up to 14 days and server logs for up to 90 days, after which they are removed automatically.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent for SMS or WhatsApp messages. You can correct most details yourself through your registration link while your registration is open; for anything else, contact us below. We respond within 30 days. These rights apply under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

Registration is for qualified nurses aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect information about children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. For significant changes, we will tell registered nurses by WhatsApp or SMS.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, message us on WhatsApp at +91 99524 07365, or contact your Uyir district office.
